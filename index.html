About Jolie Anastasia

Jolie Anastasia (née Jolie Smith) is a folk and Americana singer-songwriter whose music feels like a conversation around a fire—intimate, timeless, and rooted in the human spirit. With a voice that carries both tenderness and grit, she weaves stories of love, resilience, and belonging.

Hear Jolie on Records & Rockstars with Jeff Woods

From Ontario to open skies far beyond, Jolie’s melodies echo the traditions of the folk greats while carving her own path. She balances vulnerability with quiet strength, drawing listeners into a world where raw emotion meets poetic reflection.

Her music invites you to slow down, listen deeply, and find yourself reflected in the poetry of her songs—like letters written from the soul to anyone willing to hear.

Her lyrical gift is evident from the opening lines of ‘Lucky To Find’: “Looking at your face, and seeing through your smile. As you try to hide a trace of riveting disguise.” It’s an image at once intimate and revealing, capturing the duality of tenderness and hurt that defines much of her work.
